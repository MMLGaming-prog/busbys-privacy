Busby's Bus Jam – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Busby's Bus Jam collects as little information as possible: your game progress stays on your device, and only Google's ad, purchase and sign-in services handle data on our behalf.

This policy explains what information the mobile game Busby's Bus Jam ("the game", "we", "us") uses, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by MLL Gaming, an independent developer. By playing the game you agree to this policy.

Information the game uses

WhatWhyWhere it is kept
Game progress: levels, stars, boosters, cards, outfits, settingsTo save your game between sessionsOn your device only

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not sell personal information.

Ads are always optional: you choose when to watch one, in return for a reward. Buying Remove Ads gives those rewards without watching.

Third-party services

The game uses these Google services, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (optional reward ads): How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases) and Google Play Games Services (sign-in, cloud save): Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (game server, if used in the future): Firebase privacy and security

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's consent message.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and data deletion

  • Ads: watching an ad is always your choice. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
  • Progress on your device: uninstall the game or clear its app data in your phone's settings to delete it.
  • Cloud save, leagues and friends: email us at the address below and we will delete your data within 30 days.
  • Purchases: Google keeps purchase records as required by law; refunds go through Google Play.

Security and retention

Data sent between the game and online services is encrypted (HTTPS). We keep league and friends data only while your account is active, and delete it after 24 months without play.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
